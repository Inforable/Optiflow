# SOP-LO-08 Lube-oil pump P-104A/B inspection
> Synthetic demo SOP. Vibration zones per ISO 10816-7 Category I as reproduced by Europump.

## §1 Vibration check
Measure bearing-housing velocity (mm/s RMS). Zone A up to 2.5, zone B up to 4.0, zone C up to 6.6, above 6.6 zone D (pumps up to 200 kW).

## §2 Motor current
Compare IT-104A with the nameplate full-load current times the service factor. Current rising at constant flow and head suggests mechanical drag.

## §3 Seal check
Any visible continuous drip from the mechanical seal is a leak; photograph it.

## §4 Relief valve check
A passing relief valve lowers discharge pressure PT-106 with flat filter dP; check for return-line warmth.
