# SOP-LO-05 Standby and emergency pump auto-start test
> Synthetic demo SOP. Values are SITE SETPOINTS.

## §1 Interval
Test the auto-start of standby AC pump P-104B and DC emergency pump P-105 every 30 days and after any work on the lube-oil control circuit.

## §2 P-104B auto-start test
1. Inform the control room; turbine on load is acceptable.
2. Slowly open the test valve on the PSL-104 sensing line until the switch operates (1.2 barg).
3. Confirm relay K3 and contactor KM-104B pick up and P-104B starts within 3 seconds.
4. Restore the test valve; stop P-104B from the panel after header pressure stabilises.

## §3 P-105 DC emergency pump test
1. Check DC battery BAT-220V voltage and the charger status.
2. Operate the PSLL-104 test (0.8 barg) with the trip bypass engaged by the control room.
3. Confirm relay K5 and contactor KM-105 pick up and P-105 starts.

## §4 Record
Record start times, header pressure and battery voltage. A failed start is a safety-critical defect: inform the shift supervisor immediately.
