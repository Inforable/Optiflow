# SOP-LO-02 Duplex filter F-101A/B changeover under load
> Synthetic demo SOP. Values are SITE SETPOINTS.

## §1 When to change over
Change over when PDT-101 reaches 1.0 bar, or earlier when the header pressure PT-104 is trending down with rising filter dP. Operators follow this SOP; deviations are logged.

## §2 Precautions
- Never leave both filter elements isolated. Header pressure must stay above 1.2 barg.
- Wear gloves and eye protection; hot oil.

## §3 Changeover steps
### §3.1 Prepare the standby element
Open the fill/vent valve on F-101B until oil appears clear at the vent sight glass; close the vent.
### §3.2 Switch the transfer valve
Turn the transfer valve slowly from F-101A to F-101B while watching PT-104.
### §3.3 Confirm
Confirm PDT-101 below 0.4 bar and PT-104 recovering toward 1.6 barg. Record readings.

## §4 Inspect the removed element
Photograph the element. Sludge, emulsion or a milky film suggests water in the oil (see SOP-LO-04). Metal flakes suggest bearing distress (see SOP-LO-07).
