# SOP-LO-03 Oil cooler E-101A/B isolation and changeover
> Synthetic demo SOP. Values are SITE SETPOINTS.

## §1 Purpose
Isolate a leaking or fouled oil cooler and run on the standby cooler without interrupting oil supply.

## §2 Switch to the standby cooler
Open the oil and cooling-water valves of E-101B, then close the oil-side valves of E-101A, then its cooling-water valves. Confirm TT-102 stays below 55 degC.

## §3 Leak check
Oil-side pressure must stay above cooling-water pressure. If cooling-water pressure exceeds oil pressure across a cooler with a tube leak, water enters the oil. Compare the gauges on both sides and photograph them.

## §4 Drain the isolated cooler
Drain the oil side of E-101A to a closed container; check for free water.

## §5 Repair
Plug or retube E-101A at the next planned shutdown. Pressure-test before return to service.
