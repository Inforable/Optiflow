# SOP-LO-06 Oil purification and dehydration
> Synthetic demo SOP. Values are SITE SETPOINTS.

## §1 When to purify
When water in oil exceeds the site action limit of 500 ppm, or when the oil is hazy at visual check.

## §2 Connect the purifier
Connect the vacuum dehydrator (or centrifuge) to the reservoir side-stream connections; start at low flow.

## §3 Run
Run until water is below 500 ppm on two consecutive samples taken 4 hours apart.

## §4 Verify
Re-sample per SOP-LO-04 §2 and record the photo; oil must be clear and bright.
