# SOP-LO-07 Bearing temperature high response
> Synthetic demo SOP. Values are SITE SETPOINTS, kept below the practical babbitt limit of about 129 degC.

## §1 Alarm
TE-201/202 or TE-203/204 above 115 degC: alarm.

## §2 Check the oil supply
Check header pressure PT-104, oil supply temperature TT-102 and filter dP PDT-101. Low header pressure or hot oil reduces the oil film.

## §3 Reduce risk
Reduce load if the trend continues; prepare the standby pump; inform the shift supervisor.

## §4 Trip
Trip the turbine at 125 degC or on PSLL-104.
