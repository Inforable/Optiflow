# SOP-LO-04 Lube-oil sampling and visual checks
> Synthetic demo SOP. Visual criteria follow common turbine-oil practice (oil should be clear and bright).

## §1 Sampling points
Reservoir drain (bottom), header sample point, filter outlet.

## §2 Reservoir drain sample and photo
1. Flush the drain valve for 5 seconds into a waste container.
2. Fill a clean clear glass or PET bottle to about three quarters.
3. Hold the bottle against a light and photograph it.
4. Turbine oil in good condition is clear and bright. Hazy or milky oil indicates water. Dark oil indicates oxidation. Visible particles indicate contamination.
5. Record a short voice note describing what you see.

## §3 Hot-plate crackle test (optional)
Drop oil on a hot plate; bubbling and crackling indicate free water.

## §4 Lab sample
Send a sample for Karl Fischer water, ISO 4406 particle count, TAN and viscosity. Site action limit for water: 500 ppm (standard limit for steam turbines 0.1% = 1,000 ppm).
