# Sample media

`hazy_oil.jpg` and `clear_oil.jpg` are synthetic sample images generated by
`backend/scripts/make_sample_images.py` (same files are bundled in
`backend/data/samples/`, so in live mode Gemini judges the real image).
They are used by the "Use sample photo" buttons for judges without an oil sample.
The `.svg` files are the earlier illustrations and are no longer referenced.
For the strongest demo, take a real photo of an oil sample with "Take photo".
